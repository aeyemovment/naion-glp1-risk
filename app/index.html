NAION risk with GLP-1 receptor agonists

A research calculator built from published evidence (11 cohorts, 3.27 million reconstructed patients, 1,425 events)

Research prototype. Not for clinical, diagnostic or treatment decisions. The model was trained on study-level published data. It discriminates modestly (AUROC 0.66) and close to chance in settings it has not seen (0.53). Do not use it to start, stop or choose a drug, or to decide on antiplatelet therapy. Calculations run in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent or stored.

Even in the highest tier, absolute risk stays below about 1 in 1,000. At the top‑10% threshold the model's positive predictive value was about 0.09%: roughly 1,100 flagged patients for each NAION event. Known individual risk factors are not in the model, including a small cup‑to‑disc ratio ("disc at risk"), sleep apnoea, nocturnal hypotension and optic disc drusen.

How this works

Gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost, 300 trees) were trained on count-weighted patient-level data reconstructed from published GLP-1RA cohort studies. Inputs are applied to a reference setting (US, EHR network, NAION outcome) at the cohort-average sex mix. Age and horizon are clamped to the training range. The tier compares the GLP-1RA estimate with the top 10% of predicted risk in the training data.
